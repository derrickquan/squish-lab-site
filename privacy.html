Squish Lab

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Squish Lab is a game made by Derrick Quan and published by Apex Foods LLC. This policy explains what happens with your information when you play it on iPhone.

The short version

Squish Lab does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

Your game save

Your squishies, coins, backyard and trades are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, your save is deleted with it.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We do not use your camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

In-game trading

The traders in Squish Lab are characters in the game. You are not trading with or talking to real people, and nothing you make leaves your phone.

Purchases

Squish Lab is free and has no in-app purchases. Coins in the game are earned by playing and have no real-world value.

Children

Because Squish Lab collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version adds features that use data, such as online trading with other players, we will update this page before that version is released and say what changed.
