Squish Lab

Build squishy toys, squeeze-test them for ASMR points, grow a backyard and trade with friends.

Help

How do I get a rarer squishy?
Your squishy is scored on the 3 things it is best at, so pick parts that work well together, like a slow-rise filling with a soft skin or crunchy beads with a crunchy texture. Lucky clovers from your garden give extra chances at a great squeeze.
How do I earn coins?
Shake the money tree in your backyard, spin the prize wheel, sell squishies or trade for ones worth more.
What do accessories do?
Tap one to try it on. The one a squishy wears raises one of its test scores. Accessories belong to the squishy you buy them for.
How do I trade with a friend?
Open Trade, then Traders. Share your 6-letter friend code with a friend you know in real life, or type theirs and tap Add friend. A grown-up answers a quick question to approve each new friend. There is no chat, and you can block or report any friend.
How do I turn off friend trading?
In Trade, then Traders, tap Turn off friend trading. It erases your friend code, fun name, friends list and showcase from the internet. See the privacy policy.
Where is my game saved?
On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes your save.
